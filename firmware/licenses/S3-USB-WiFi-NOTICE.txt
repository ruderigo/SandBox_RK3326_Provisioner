FireFly S3 USB Wi-Fi (firmware/s3_usb_wifi/s3-usb-wifi-<version>.bin)

The firmware's own code (s3_usb_wifi/main/ in this repository) is MIT, as is
the rest of the RK3326 Provisioner (LICENSE at the top of the repository).
Its full source, and the exact sources of everything below that is built
into the image, are in s3_usb_wifi/ (vendored components) and in ESP-IDF
v5.3.2 (https://github.com/espressif/esp-idf/tree/v5.3.2).

Built into the image, each under its own licence:

  Espressif's Wi-Fi and PHY libraries (Apache-2.0) and mbedTLS (Apache-2.0)
- lwIP (in ESP-IDF): BSD-3-Clause (lwIP-LICENSE.txt)
- FreeRTOS kernel (in ESP-IDF): MIT (FreeRTOS-LICENSE.txt)
- esp_tinyusb 3.0.0, Espressif Systems: Apache-2.0 (Apache-2.0.txt),
  from https://github.com/espressif/esp-usb commit 6a577e5c946edefdcefc45af94ded4e5f2f5ed0f
